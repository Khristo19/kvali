# Field validation

Nothing is confirmed yet. These conversations turn the idea into evidence. Write the answers down; the numbers go in the deck.

## 1. DJI distributor (week 1)

Goal: a partner, a drone, an operator, and farm intros.

- How many Agras units have you sold in Georgia? How many are actively working?
- What do owners say is the hardest part: finding jobs, getting paid, regulation?
- Would a job pipeline help you sell more drones? Would you offer it with each sale?
- Can we borrow one drone and one certified operator for a demo job in week 4?
- Which farms could we approach? Which crops, which months?
- Would you collect lari from farmers and settle USDC underneath? (the "DePIN mullet")
- What spray data do customers export today (SmartFarm, controller records)?
- **Prices for the drone-vs-tractor slide:** a new 50–90 hp tractor plus an orchard/boom sprayer in Georgia (₾), and what farmers pay to hire tractor spraying per hectare (₾/ha).

**Ask to leave with:** one named contact, a date for a field visit, a drone for week 4.

## 2. Operators (2–3 conversations)

- How do you find jobs today? How many hours did you bill last season?
- How do you price: per hectare, per hour? Typical rate in lari? What does tractor spraying cost farmers per hectare in your area?
- Have you had payment disputes? How were they resolved?
- Would you lock a bond equal to the job value to get verified jobs? At what size does it hurt?
- Can you export liters sprayed per flight? Show me.
- Do you hold GCAA certification? Pesticide licence? Insurance?

## 3. Farmers (3–5 conversations)

- How many hectares, which crop, how many spray passes per season?
- How do you find an operator today? What went wrong last time?
- How do you know the field was actually sprayed at the right rate?
- Do you need spray records for a buyer, exporter or certification?
- Would you pay through the distributor, in lari, if it came with a verified record?

## 4. Exporters / buyers (1–2 conversations, for the records product)

- Do EU buyers ask for chemical-application records? In what form?
- Who produces them today, and how much do you trust them?
- Would a verifiable record per field per season be worth paying for? Per hectare?

## 5. Validator seats (weeks 1–2)

Goal: three organisations, one per seat, willing to be v1 validators. See DECISIONS.md D9.

| Seat | Who to ask |
|---|---|
| Operator side | DJI distributor's agronomist or technical team |
| Farmer side | Georgian Farmers' Association, or a Kakheti farming co-op |
| Neutral | Agricultural University of Georgia (agronomy), or a hazelnut/wine exporter's quality team |

- Would you check spray records and, occasionally, visit a field to place and read spray cards?
- What would a field visit cost you in time and travel? (We assume ~$30; check.)
- Is 1% of each job for data checks, plus ~$30 per field visit, fair pay?
- Could you rule on a dispute within 48 hours of a challenge?
- Any conflict of interest we should know about?
