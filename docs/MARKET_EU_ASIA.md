# Market and impact evidence: Asia and Europe spray drones

Compiled 2026-10-10. [verified] = source page opened; [secondary] = seen only in a search summary or aggregator. "Vendor" = DJI/XAG claim, not independent.

## 10 pitch-ready numbers

1. More than 600,000 farm drones were flying worldwide at end of 2025, up from 400,000 a year earlier, in 100+ countries (DJI, vendor). 2025. [verified] https://dronexl.co/2026/04/30/dji-agricultural-drones-600000-eu-aerial-spray-italy-decree/
2. China alone had 251,000 farm-service drones in 2024. 2024. [verified] https://subsites.chinadaily.com.cn/wic/2025-03/07/c_1076118.htm
3. Those drones covered 2.67 billion mu of farmland (about 178 million hectares), up nearly 25% in a year. 2024. [verified, same Xinhua/China Daily page; hectare conversion is ours, 15 mu = 1 ha]
4. Drones worldwide have treated over 500 million hectares by mid-2024 (DJI, vendor). 2024. [secondary] https://dronedj.com/2024/07/10/dji-drone-farming-agriculture-report/
5. Rice trial in India: a drone sprayed 9 acres in 1.2 hours; knapsack spraying took 34.3 hours (about 28 times faster). 2025, peer-reviewed, one field. [verified] https://horizonepublishing.com/journals/index.php/PST/article/view/7758
6. Same trial: 72 litres of water by drone against 240 litres by hand, one third. 2025. [verified, same link]
7. Vietnam's Mekong Delta: drone spraying price fell from about 180,000 to 120,000 dong per hectare (US$6.80 to US$4.60) as operators multiplied; one network runs 6,000 drone teams. 2026. [verified] https://vietnamnews.vn/society/1780879/drones-reshape-agriculture-but-challenges-remain.html
8. Thailand: over 10,000 registered farm drones (about 4 million ha, 30% of cropland), while a government survey says 80,000 would be needed. 2023. [secondary] https://mddb.apec.org/Documents/2023/TPTWG/AEG-TM1/23_tptwg_aeg_tm1_010.pdf (conflicts with 9,063 figure, see Asia table)
9. Europe is opening up: the EU Commission proposed (Dec 2025) a drone exception to the aerial-spray ban; Member States backed it on 27 May 2026; the Commission would have 30 months to name eligible drone types. [verified] https://www.eunews.it/en/2026/05/27/the-eu-council-approves-simplifying-pesticide-regulations-and-facilitates-drone-usage/
10. EU law already obliges every professional sprayer to keep application records for at least 3 years (Reg. 1107/2009 Art. 67); electronic transfer of records was pushed to 1 Jan 2027. 2025. [secondary] https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107/article/67/data.htm

## 4. Marketplace pain points

- Fragmented smallholders cannot buy drones (about US$10k in Zimbabwe; PhilRice cites P0.5-1M); service model fills the gap [secondary] https://techcabal.com/2022/04/13/zimbabwes-farm-buzz/ , https://www.philrice.gov.ph/will-pay-spray-sky/
- Price war: Vietnam fell about 33% in a few years (item 7). Benin farmers will pay only $5-10/ha (177 cotton and 232 rice farmers) [secondary] https://www.climate-chance.org/en/best-pratices/drones-for-precision-agriculture-smart-digital-solutions-for-improving-rice-and-pineapple-productivity-in-benin/
- Crashes can cost over VND70M (~$2,700); parts take 5-30 days; some teams rinse tanks in canals [verified, Vietnam News link]. Vietnam's Decree 288 (Nov 2025) requires registration, pilot licence and flight logs.
- Thailand: "no standard for the service"; Department of Agriculture issues ID cards and a best-practice guide; supply of providers below demand [secondary].
- Payback: one Thai rice study put service payback at about one year (Thai-language, unopened); US extension says payback "hard to pin down" [secondary].
- Informal cash payment, disputes, missing records: NO evidence found. State as our hypothesis, not a fact.

## 5. Demand for verifiable records

- EU Reg. 1107/2009 Art. 67: users keep records 3+ years (product, dose, date, area, crop); retailers can request access via the authority. Digital format Reg. 2023/564, deferral Reg. 2025/2203 to 1 Jan 2027 [secondary] https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2009/1107/article/67/data.htm
- GLOBALG.A.P.: complete application records for every product, kept 2+ years, available at audit [verified] https://academy.globalgap.org/academy/data/gglive/lm_data/lm_3917/eCourse_AllFarmBase/html/05_02_RecordingSystem.html . Mandatory MRL result reporting for GGN-labelled produce. Some German retailers cap residue count (older, unverified).
- Italy and the EU drone derogations require authorisation plus reporting to EFSA/Commission within 30 days of each authorisation: records are the gate to legal drone work.
- EUDR is about deforestation and not pesticides; do not use it. No insurance or subsidy programme requiring application records was confirmed (Korea pays 120,000 won/ha for joint aerial spraying; Namo Drone Didi subsidy; both imply proof of work, but terms unverified).

## Don't say

- "Drones save 90% water", "30-40% chemical" as fact: vendor or unsourced; one trial says one third water.
- Any hectare or fleet number as "ministry data": none found; all China and global totals trace to Xinhua reporting or DJI.
- "173M ha sprayed": includes non-spraying work; and conflicts with 178M ha.
- Japan 2023 "1.2M ha", Vietnam fleet counts, Thailand 10,000: weak or conflicting.
- "Cheaper than manual" everywhere: Hubei and one Thai study say otherwise.
- "Safer for operators" as measured; "higher yields"; "EU allows drone spraying" (not yet; proposal only).
- "Farmers lose money to cash disputes": no evidence found.
- Market size/regional shares without naming the firm; Europe 30% share is unreliable.
- Spain, Austria, Netherlands, Hungary, Romania, Turkey, Central Asia: no data.
